About me

I’m a 19-year-old engineering student who is curious to understand the world and improve the quality of life for others. For me, success is about building a strong mindset, learning from new perspectives, connecting with driven people, and using what I know to make a real impact.

I’m currently studying Engineering Science on the Biomedical Engineering track at UofT. Outside of classes, I’m a recruit on the Electrical sub-team for UofT Formula Racing. I’m interested in the intersection of healthcare, tech and sustainability, and I'm currently spending a lot of time exploring longevity research and how stem cells can impact human epigenetic aging.

When I’m not studying or working on a project, I’m a competitive taekwondo athlete and vocalist. I also love cycling, photography, a good book, and traveling.

Research Paper
Vitreous Floaters
To figure out how eye floaters affect young adults, I conducted a global study exploring the association between floater severity and psychological distress. After discovering a moderate connection with perceived stress, my research highlights the need for doctors to look beyond just the physical symptoms and offer better mental health support and non-surgical treatments for affected youth.

Biomarker Diagnosis
Switch Health Screening Test
Partnering with Switch Health, my team and I proposed an at-home prostate cancer screening kit that utilizes AMACR protein biomarkers in semen for highly accurate detection. This solution offers a cost-effective, non-invasive alternative to traditional exams, breaking down the stigma surrounding prostate screening to drive earlier and life-saving diagnoses.

Longevity Article
Secret to Living Longer
I wrote an article breaking down the nine biological hallmarks of aging and the science working to reverse them. My hope is to make complex longevity research into accessible insights and share everyday lifestyle habits that anyone can adopt right to slow down their own aging process.

Moonshot Idea
Vaccigenex
My team developed Vaccigenex, a CRISPR-based vaccine designed to cure familial hypercholesterolemia (FH) and reduce patients' risks of premature cardiovascular disease. By utilizing lipid nanoparticles to deliver Cas9 directly to liver cells, our gene-editing approach aims to eradicate the root genetic mutation.
